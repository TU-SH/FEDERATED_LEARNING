# FL SURVEYS AND REVIEWS
- Aledhari et al. (2020): Federated Learning: A Survey on Enabling Technologies, Protocols, and Applications (https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/stamp/stamp.jsp?arnumber=9153560) - reviews protocols, architecture platforms, and real-life use-cases in FL
- Zhang et al. (2021): A survey on federated learning (https://lhkhiem28.github.io/paperhub/Surveys/A%20Survey%20on%20Federated%20Learning.pdf) - surveys FL on the basis of ata partitioning, privacy mechanism,
machine learning model, communication architecture and systems heterogeneity along with current challenges and future research directions

- Yurdem et al. (2024): Federated learning: Overview, strategies, applications, tools andfuture directions (https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2405844024141680) - reviews FL categorisation (VFL, HFL), algorithms and there comparison, security concerns, FL applications, frameworks and tools, opportunities, challenges and future directions.
